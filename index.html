JARVIS

A personal voice assistant

JARVIS is a private, self-hosted voice assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: it has a single user, the person who set it up, and no one else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission, JARVIS reads and manages the owner's Google Calendar on request — for example "What's on my calendar tomorrow?" or "Move the dentist to Friday at 10".
